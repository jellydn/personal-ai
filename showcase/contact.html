Before you reach out

What to expect

A few honest answers, so you know what happens after you hit send.

Have a bug in a project?

Open an issue on that specific repository — not here. Include the steps to reproduce and what you expected to happen. A clear report is the fastest way to get a fix.

Will you reply to everything?

Honestly — every message gets read, but replies are triaged. Issues and direct emails usually get an answer within a few days; long-form questions may take longer. The tracker itself is a one-person project.

Can I contribute?

Yes — open an issue to discuss an idea first, or jump straight to a pull request. The tracker welcomes improvements, and each project repo lists how to run it.

Every project on this site is real, runnable, and open source — no sign-ups, no paywalls.
